1.WHO WE ARE

Free PDF Editor - All PDF Tools (“the App”) is an Android application for opening, creating, editing, annotating and converting PDF files. The App is published on Google Play by indoon0227 (“we”, “us”, “our”).

This policy explains what information the App accesses, what is collected, how it is used and shared, how long it is kept, and how you can delete it. It applies to the App only. It does not apply to other apps, websites or services you may reach from the App.

2.SUMMARY

  • There is no account, no sign-in and no user profile.
  • Your PDF files, photos, Word files and recognized text are processed on your device. We do not upload your documents to a server we run, and we do not operate a server that stores your documents.
  • The App displays banner advertising supplied by Google AdMob. To deliver ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects certain device and usage information as described in section 5.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

3.INFORMATION PROCESSED ONLY ON YOUR DEVICE

The following stays on your device and is not transmitted to us:

a) Files you choose
PDF, image and Word (.docx) files you select with the Android system file picker are copied into the App’s private storage so they can be opened. Conversion, editing, annotation and text recognition (OCR) run on your device’s processor.

b) Working data
The most recent document, temporary conversion drafts, notes, page annotations and one undo copy are stored in the App’s private storage. They exist only so you can resume work after closing or reopening the App.

c) Settings
Your interface language and text-recognition language choices are stored locally on your device.

d) Text recognition
OCR uses Google ML Kit models that are bundled inside the App. The models are already on your device, so recognition runs offline and your documents, photos and recognized text are not uploaded for recognition.

This working data is excluded from Android backup and device-to-device transfer. You can erase it at any time (see section 9).

4.PERMISSIONS THE APP REQUESTS

  • Internet and network state: required only so the advertising SDK can request and display ads.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) and related advertising services permissions: used by the advertising SDK as described in section 5.
  • Wake lock and foreground service: used by the advertising SDK while it fetches an ad.

The App does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, SMS, call logs, calendar, health data, or your full file system. File access is limited to the specific items you pick with the system file picker.

5.ADVERTISING AND THIRD-PARTY DATA COLLECTION

The App shows banner ads through Google AdMob. AdMob is provided by Google. When an ad is requested, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares the following data automatically, for advertising, analytics and fraud-prevention purposes:

Data typeWhat is collected
IP addressYour device’s IP address, which may be used to estimate the general (city-level) location of the device
User product interactionsApp launches, taps and video views
Diagnostic informationApp launch time, hang rate, energy usage and similar performance data
Device and account identifiersAndroid advertising ID (AD ID), app set ID, and other identifiers related to accounts signed in on the device, where applicable

All data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS.

Who receives it: Google LLC and its advertising partners, as described in Google’s own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

We do not receive your documents, photos, recognized text, or the contents of your files through the advertising SDK. We receive only aggregated ad performance reports from AdMob, such as impressions and estimated revenue.

Your choices:

  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, and turn off personalized advertising, in Android Settings > Google > Ads.
  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, where the law requires it, you must give consent before your personal data is used to personalize ads. You can withdraw consent at any time using the same Android settings, or by contacting us.
  • Limiting or disabling the advertising ID does not remove ads; it may make them less relevant.

Cookies: the App itself does not use browser cookies. The advertising SDK may use local storage and identifiers on your device for the purposes described above.

6.WHAT WE DO NOT DO

  • We do not require or offer account registration.
  • We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, postal address or payment details inside the App.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not use your documents or recognized text to train machine learning models.
  • We do not show ads based on the content of your documents.

7.LEGAL BASES FOR PROCESSING (EEA, UK, SWITZERLAND)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on:

  • Consent, for the use of your personal data for ad personalization where consent is required, and for the storage of identifiers on your device where required. You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate interests, for serving non-personalized advertising, preventing fraud and abuse, and keeping the App secure and stable.
  • Legal obligation, where we must retain or disclose information to comply with applicable law.

8.YOUR RIGHTS

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal data held about you;
  • request correction or deletion;
  • request restriction of, or object to, processing;
  • data portability;
  • withdraw consent at any time;
  • lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

California residents (CCPA/CPRA) have the right to know what is collected, to request deletion, to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, and not to be discriminated against for exercising those rights. We do not sell personal information as defined by the CCPA.

Because the App has no account and we operate no server that stores your documents, we generally hold no document data about you to disclose or delete. Requests concerning advertising data should be directed to Google using the links in section 5. You may also contact us at indoon0227@gmail.com and we will respond within the period required by applicable law.

9.RETENTION AND DELETION

  • On-device working data is kept until you delete it. There is no automatic expiry that sends it anywhere.
  • To delete it: open the App, tap “About” (앱 안내), then choose “Delete working data and cache”. This removes the stored document, notes, drafts, the undo copy and temporary files.
  • Uninstalling the App removes all data in its private storage.
  • PDF files you exported to another location are outside the App’s control and must be deleted by you using your file manager or the storage provider you chose.
  • Data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is retained by Google according to Google’s privacy policy.

10.SECURITY

  • Working data is stored in the App’s private storage area, which other apps cannot read on a standard Android device.
  • App data is excluded from Android backup and device transfer.
  • Data collected by the advertising SDK is encrypted in transit with TLS.
  • No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we limit collection to what is necessary for the App and its advertising to function.

11.INTERNATIONAL DATA TRANSFERS

We do not transfer your documents outside your device. Data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own, including the United States, where Google and its partners operate. Google states that it applies appropriate safeguards for such transfers, as described in the Google Privacy Policy linked in section 5.

12.CHILDREN

The App is intended for users aged 18 and over. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App has no account, registration or contact form, so no personal information is knowingly received from any user, including children.

If you believe a child has used the App in a way that raises a privacy concern, contact us at indoon0227@gmail.com and we will take appropriate action, including advising you how to delete the App’s on-device working data.
