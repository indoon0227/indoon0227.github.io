2.THE SERVICE

The App provides the following features on Android devices. The scope and the known limits of these features are also shown in the App under "About".

2.1 Features provided

  • View PDF — open a PDF, zoom in and out, move between pages
  • Photos to PDF — convert 1 to 30 photos into a single PDF
  • Text to PDF — create an A4 PDF from typed text (up to 40,000 characters)
  • Word (.docx) to PDF — text-focused body conversion (up to 25 MB per file)
  • OCR text recognition — Korean, English, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, Vietnamese, Portuguese and Spanish; 1 to 10 pages or photos per run
  • Annotations — pen, highlighter, text notes, signature, undo and redo
  • Page editing — rotate, delete, reorder pages and merge documents
  • Saving — the original is preserved and the result is saved as a copy
  • Language settings — 7 interface languages and 7 OCR languages

2.2 Limitations

The following are deliberate exclusions or technical limits of the App. We do not warrant the accuracy or completeness of any output affected by these limits.

  • OCR area editing works only on horizontal text that is isolated on a plain white background. Applying it re-saves the whole page as an image, so text selection, search, tags and accessibility information may be lost. Tables, stamps and drawings near the edit may be erased — always check the preview before applying.
  • Original-text editing supports only simple horizontal text blocks on pages that are not rotated. A font similar to the original is estimated and used; this is not exact font restoration. Paragraph reflow is not supported.
  • Word conversion is text-focused. Tables are flattened into rows, and images, charts, equations, headers, footnotes and layout are not included. Legacy .doc files, macro-enabled or encrypted Word files, and Excel or web document conversion are not supported.
  • The signature feature places a hand-drawn image on the document. It is not a certificate-based digital signature and cannot be used where a legally valid signature is required.
  • Editing is not supported for encrypted PDFs, digitally signed PDFs, text inside form XObjects, tagged documents, or vertical or slanted text.
  • Saving an edit may invalidate existing digital signatures.
  • OCR is text recognition, not translation. Changing the interface language does not translate your document.
  • Emoji and some special characters may not display or save correctly.
  • The maximum size of any file you import is 100 MB.

2.3 Where processing happens

Conversion, editing and recognition run on your device. We do not operate a server that stores your documents. See the Privacy Policy for details.
